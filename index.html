What Kalodata is, before the price question

Kalodata presents itself as a tool for TikTok Shop analytics and insights, with the modules named straight across the top: category, shop, creator, product, video and ad, and livestream. Its own FAQ describes it as a data analytics and insights tool for TikTok Shop, aimed at sellers, brands, creators and affiliates who want trending products, influencers to work with, video inspiration, livestream ideas, market research and competitive analysis in one place. Three AI products sit alongside it: Kaloboost, described as an AI-driven affiliate platform, Kaloclip as an all-in-one AI content platform for ecommerce, and kalopilot as an AI agent for market and business insights. That is a lot of surface area, and it is all pointed at one channel.

The numbers the site does publish

Kalodata leads with dataset size rather than plan tiers: more than two hundred million product records, more than two hundred and fifty million creator records, more than four hundred million video and livestream records, and a thousand days of historical data. Those figures are the company's own and they describe coverage, not accuracy, but the history depth is the one I would weigh most. Seasonal product research is hard to do well with a shallow archive, and a thousand days covers several cycles of the same category. The entry point advertised is a free seven-day trial with no credit card needed, which makes the evaluation genuinely cheap and removes most of the excuse for guessing at fit from the outside.

Kalodata pricing: what the homepage leaves out

Actual plan prices are not printed on the page I read, so I am not going to invent a monthly figure. Check the official pricing page and, while you are there, answer three questions for yourself. Which modules does the tier you are considering unlock, because suites like this commonly gate creator or ad data separately. How many seats are included, since agency use falls over on that detail more often than on price. And what the export limits are, because a plan that shows you everything and lets you export very little changes how the tool fits into a workflow. Use the free trial to test those three things specifically rather than browsing the dashboard.

Where Reachara fits differently

Reachara is not a TikTok Shop analytics suite and pretending otherwise would waste your time. It does competitor ad monitoring and creator or influencer discovery, which is a narrower job aimed at people running acquisition rather than people managing a marketplace catalogue. If the question you ask most weeks is which creatives a rival launched and who is promoting them, that is the tool shaped for it. If the question is which products are trending in a category and which livestreams converted, Kalodata's dataset is the one with the answer. Plenty of teams honestly need both, in which case this stops being a comparison and becomes a budget conversation about which one you cannot work without.

Choosing by the question you ask

Marketplace research

Trending products, category movement, livestream performance and a thousand days of history all point to Kalodata, because that kind of channel-specific data has to come from a channel-specific tool.

Ad and creator hunting

Watching competitor creatives and finding the right creators to approach is Reachara's stated job, and paying for a full marketplace analytics suite is an expensive way to approximate it.

Both, for an agency

Teams running acquisition for marketplace sellers often end up wanting one of each. Trial both before deciding anything, and let a week of real questions split the budget for you.
